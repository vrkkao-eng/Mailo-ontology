Medical AI Legal Ontology (MAILO): Description back to ToC

Version 5.2.2 (patch): one substantive post-Omnibus correction, the modelling items v5.2.1 deferred, and a set of provenance and metadata repairs. UNLIKE v5.2.1, THIS RELEASE DOES ADD A CLASS AND PROPERTIES: mailo:EPOProceeding, two HighRiskRoute instances for the Annex I Section A and Section B split, and four AI Act article nodes. HEADLINE. Article 1(9) of Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 deleted AI Act Art.10(5) and Article 1(6) inserted a new Art.4a. This is not a relocation: the legal basis for processing special categories of personal data for bias detection and correction was extracted from the high-risk training-data article into Chapter I, and its personal scope was broadened from providers of high-risk systems to deployers of high-risk systems and to providers and deployers of other AI systems and AI models, on five cumulative conditions and with no obligation to perform bias detection. Art.4a is encoded as an article node and Art.10 records the deletion, so any constraint anchored to Art.10(5) is visibly without a source. As an insertion effective 27 July 2026 it cannot operate retroactively. DEFERRED ITEMS NOW CLOSED. (a) Annex I is no longer treated as a single set: AnnexISectionARoute and AnnexISectionBRoute are declared, the MDR and IVDR are recorded as Section A legislation, and MDR Annex VIII Rule 11 now triggers the Section A route. The distinction is load-bearing for timing, since Chapter III Sections 1-3 apply to Section A from 2 August 2028, a one-year deferral rather than the two years granted to Annex III. (b) Art.43 is encoded with the notified-body transitional period: the second subparagraph of Art.43(3) sets 28 January 2028, while recital 18 describes eighteen months from 27 July 2026, which would be 27 January 2028; the operative date is the Article's and both are recorded because the divergence is in the published text. (c) Art.27 is encoded as a provision, the graph having previously carried its FRIA date without the article itself. (d) Arts.53 and 55 are added for temporal completeness; no MAILO constraint depends on the GPAI regime. METADATA AND PROVENANCE. The ECLIs for C-201/14, C-311/18 and C-511/18 were withheld in v5.2.0 rather than supplied from memory, since asserting an identifier from recall is the error class that produced the original C-203/22 defect; they are now added, each verified against InfoCuria or EUR-Lex and an independent secondary source, and they pass the citation-chronology constraint. The misspelled class mailo:EPOProceding is deprecated in favour of mailo:EPOProceeding, declared equivalent, with instances typed against both; renaming a published IRI would break every reference to it. P_634_004 is marked unanchored, and the art22ExceptionApplied comment, which had presented that reading as a holding of C-634/21, is reworded. Two triggeredBy edges left doubled by the v5.2.1 simplification were removed with their orphaned amendment nodes, and three dangling nodes were connected: Sanofi and Amgen as parties to UPC_Sanofi_Amgen, and EP 3 435 866 to UPC_Dexcom_Abbott. STILL OPEN. The unverified substantive assertions on UPC_10x_NanoString, UPC_Dexcom_Abbott and UPC_Sanofi_Amgen; the holding recorded for P_Bara_001; the conceptual confusion flagged on Tension_BlackBox_PatentProtection, which treats patent protection as an incentive to secrecy where a patent in fact requires disclosure; and independent expert review, which no finding in this or either preceding corrective release has received.
