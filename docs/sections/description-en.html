Medical AI Legal Ontology (MAILO): Description back to ToC

Version 5.1.0: corrective release consolidating four rounds of work, none previously deployed. (1) All nine CJEU paragraph citations were re-checked and all nine corrected; two substantive mischaracterisations were removed (the health-status exclusion is categorical under PNR Directive Art.6(4), not curable by a GDPR Art.9(2) basis; the strict-necessity test does not entail invalidity, the Court having upheld the Directive by Charter-conforming interpretation). (2) The patent, EPO and UPC instances were verified: T 1191/19 re-dated and its holding rewritten, T 0161/18 corrected to a refused application, the citesPrecedent graph repaired, litigation statuses refreshed. (3) Legislative citations were audited: all four EHDS articles carried proposal numbering and were renumbered, Art.73 was added, PLD Art.11 was corrected from the causation presumption to exemption from liability, DGA Art.31 was corrected to Art.29, and AI Act Art.50 was relabelled. (4) MAILO's boundaries were made explicit: every case now declares an InclusionBasis and every principle an EncodingBasis, separating what a court held from what the ontology designer inferred or operationalised. Correcting the hierarchy that let the boundary errors pass, AutomatedDecisionSystem is no longer a subclass of MedicalAISystem — the entailment ran backwards, making every automated decision system medical. Parts IV and V of the SHACL shapes now enforce citation integrity and boundary declaration on the graph itself, and each shape has been tested against the error it was written for.
