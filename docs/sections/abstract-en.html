Abstract

MAILO (Medical AI Legal Ontology) is a machine-executable knowledge graph encoding EU regulatory obligations for medical artificial intelligence systems. It formalises eight EU legislative instruments -- the GDPR, MDR, AI Act, Product Liability Directive, EHDS, Data Governance Act, Data Act, and Trade Secrets Directive -- as OWL 2 classes and SHACL shape constraints, grounded in three CJEU judgments on automated decision-making (Ligue des droits humains, SCHUFA Holding, and Dun & Bradstreet Austria). MAILO functions as a deterministic, auditable compliance-verification layer: SHACL NodeShapes validate instance data describing a medical AI system against the encoded legal constraints and produce structured, traceable conformance reports.
