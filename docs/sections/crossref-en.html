InclusionBasisc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://w3id.org/mailo#InclusionBasis

Why a case is present in MAILO at all. Added in v5.1.0 after an audit found two cases carrying medical-AI technology tags they did not warrant. The underlying defect was not the tags: it was that no instance recorded WHY it had been included, so twenty implicit inclusion judgements sat unstated across the graph and could not be reviewed. Note that MAILO's method is transposition. None of the three CJEU automated-decision cases is a medical-AI dispute: C-817/19 concerns passenger name records, C-634/21 and C-203/22 concern credit scoring. A criterion requiring direct medical-AI subject matter would exclude the trilogy the ontology is built on. The criterion therefore records the BASIS for relevance rather than demanding a single subject matter.
is in range of
has inclusion basis op
has members
Direct subject matter ni, Domain context ni, Procedural precedent ni, Transposed principle ni
